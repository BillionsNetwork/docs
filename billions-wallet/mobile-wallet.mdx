## Overview

The Billions Mobile Wallet is a privacy-first, self-sovereign identity wallet for iOS and Android. It enables users to manage verified credentials, authenticate with issuers and verifiers, and generate zero-knowledge proofs — all from their mobile device, with no intermediaries and no centralized authority.

<Info>
  On mobile, ZK circuit files are downloaded to the device and proof generation happens fully on-device. No personal data leaves the user's hardware during the proof generation process.
</Info>

## Credential Sync

Credentials sync automatically via end-to-end encrypted cloud storage across the web and mobile wallet. Sign in with the same account — Google, Apple ID, or crypto wallet — and any credentials already claimed on one platform are immediately available on the other. No need to re-claim them on the app.
