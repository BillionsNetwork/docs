## Overview

The Billions Wallet App is a privacy-first, self-sovereign identity wallet for iOS and Android. It enables users to manage verified credentials, authenticate with issuers and verifiers, and generate zero-knowledge proofs — all from their mobile device, with no intermediaries and no centralized authority.

## Verification Journey

The following walkthrough describes the end-to-end experience for claiming a credential on the mobile app.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the App">
    Download the Billions Wallet App from the App Store or Google Play and open it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign In">
    Sign in with a crypto wallet. An identity is automatically derived from the authenticated account and storage keys are generated.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Identity Loads">
    The app loads the user's identity wallet and retrieves any existing credentials from encrypted cloud storage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View Available Credentials">
    The user sees the credentials available to claim in the wallet. Each credential shows its type, issuer, and status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim a Credential">
    The user selects a credential and initiates the claiming flow — for example, completing a uniqueness check for Proof of Uniqueness. The issuer verifies the user and issues the signed credential directly to the wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Credential Stored">
    The issued credential is encrypted and stored in cloud storage. It is now available for use in any verification flow — on-chain or off-chain — without needing to be claimed again.
  </Step>
</Steps>
