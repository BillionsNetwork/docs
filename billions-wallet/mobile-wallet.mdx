## Overview

The Mobile Wallet puts the same identity in your pocket. The iOS and Android apps handle credential management, issuer and verifier authentication, and on-device zero-knowledge proof generation, so you can verify who you are wherever you go — no browser, no intermediaries.

<Info>
  On mobile, ZK circuit files are downloaded to the device and proof generation happens fully on-device. No personal data leaves the user's hardware during the proof generation process.
</Info>
