The Billions Wallet holds your identity on the Billions Network. When you sign in, it creates a decentralized identifier (DID) from a key pair that only you control. Every credential you claim, such as proof that you're a unique human or that you hold a government ID, is stored in the wallet, encrypted under your keys.

When an app asks you to prove something, the wallet answers with a zero-knowledge proof. The app learns that the claim is true, for example "this person holds a Verified Human credential", without seeing the data behind it.

## What you can do with it

**Claim credentials about yourself.** Each one is claimed once and reused in every later request:

- [Proof of Uniqueness](/billions-wallet/proof-of-uniqueness): a one-time face scan that proves you're a real, unique human.
- [Verified Human](/billions-wallet/verified-human): proof of identity from a government document, such as an NFC passport or Aadhaar.
- Email and social accounts: proof that you own an email address, or a Discord, X or Telegram account.

**Prove things to apps.** Sign in to an app with your DID instead of a password, or show an app that you hold a credential. You approve each request in the wallet and see exactly what's being asked.

**Pair an AI agent with yourself.** Your Proof of Uniqueness credential is also what links an AI agent to you. See [Agents](/agents/introduction).

## How it protects your data

Your keys never leave your control. Credentials are stored encrypted, and proofs are generated in your browser or on your phone, so your personal data isn't sent anywhere to create them. An app receives a proof, not the credential. [How the Wallet Works](/billions-wallet/how-the-wallet-works) has the details.

## For developers

Apps can use the wallet in two ways:

- **Sign users in** with their DID, like a social login without a central provider. See [Login with Billions](/billions-wallet/login-with-billions).
- **Verify a credential** on your backend before granting access or a reward. See [Verify Proof of Uniqueness](/billions-wallet/verify-proof-of-uniqueness) and [Verify Verified Human](/billions-wallet/verify-verified-human).

To see both from the user's side, try the live demo at [demo.billions.network](https://demo.billions.network).
