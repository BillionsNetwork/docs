The Billions Wallet holds your identity on the Billions Network. When you sign in, it creates a decentralized identifier (DID) from a key pair that only you control. Every credential you claim, such as proof that you're a unique human or that you hold a government ID, is stored in the wallet, encrypted under your keys.

When an app asks you to prove something, the wallet answers with a zero-knowledge proof. The app learns that the claim is true, for example "this person holds a Verified Human credential", without seeing the data behind it.

## Credentials

Each credential is claimed once and reused in every later request. Your Proof of Uniqueness credential is also what links an AI agent to you. See [Agents](/agents/introduction).

- **Overview:** [Credentials](/billions-wallet/credentials). What each credential proves and how it's claimed, including proof that you own an email address, or a Discord, X or Telegram account.
- **Guide:** [Claim Proof of Uniqueness](/billions-wallet/proof-of-uniqueness). A one-time face scan that proves you're a real, unique human.
- **Guide:** [Claim Verified Human](/billions-wallet/verified-human). Proof of identity from a government document, such as an NFC passport or Aadhaar.

## Integrate

Apps can use the wallet in two ways. The user approves each request in the wallet and sees exactly what's being asked.

- **Sign users in** with their DID instead of a password, like a social login without a central provider. See [Login with Billions](/billions-wallet/login-with-billions).
- **Verify a credential** on your backend before granting access or a reward. See [Verify Proof of Uniqueness](/billions-wallet/verify-proof-of-uniqueness) and [Verify Verified Human](/billions-wallet/verify-verified-human).

To see both from the user's side, try the live demo at [demo.billions.network](https://demo.billions.network). [How Verification Works](/billions-wallet/how-verification-works) explains what happens between the request and your backend's check.

## How it protects your data

Your keys never leave your control. Credentials are stored encrypted, and proofs are generated in your browser or on your phone, so your personal data isn't sent anywhere to create them. An app receives a proof, not the credential. [How the Wallet Works](/billions-wallet/how-the-wallet-works) has the details.
