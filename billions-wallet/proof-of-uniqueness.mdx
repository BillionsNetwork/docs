## Why Proof of Uniqueness

Proof of Uniqueness (PoU) verifies that each participant in a project is a real, unique human — not a bot, and not one operator running many accounts.

In airdrops, token distributions, and other reward programs, projects want rewards to reach real, contributing users. Bots and Sybil attacks — a single operator running hundreds of accounts — exploit these programs and lead to unfair resource allocation. PoU closes that gap: one face maps to exactly one credential, and one credential maps to exactly one on-chain identity.

## Claim your credential

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the wallet">
    Open the Billions Wallet — on the web at [wallet.billions.network](https://wallet.billions.network) or through the mobile app — and sign in with Google, Apple ID, or a crypto wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Uniqueness credential">
    From the credentials dashboard, find the **Uniqueness** credential option and select it to start the claim flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete face verification">
    Follow the on-screen prompts to complete a one-time face scan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Credential issued">
    Once verification succeeds, the credential is issued directly to your wallet, encrypted, and stored for reuse. You won't need to repeat the face scan — every future request for Proof of Uniqueness, including [linking an AI agent's identity](/agents/identity-skill), reuses this same credential automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Already claimed a Proof of Uniqueness credential? Any new flow that asks for it — including agent linking — verifies against your existing credential instead of asking you to scan your face again.
</Info>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What are the best practices for a successful face scan?">
    For the best results:

    - Use the latest version of the Billions app.
    - Verify in a well-lit environment with a neutral background.
    - Keep your face uncovered — no glasses, headphones, scarves, or hats.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title='What does "Face not unique" mean?'>
    This appears during the claim process when the facial biometrics you provided are already registered to an existing Billions account — only one Proof of Uniqueness credential can be claimed per person. Log in with the original account where your face was first verified instead of claiming a new one.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
