## Overview

Billions is the first **Human and AI network** — a privacy-preserving identity and trust platform that lets you prove you're real without revealing who you are. Built by the creators of Polygon, Hermez, and Disco.xyz, it powers over 9,000 zero-knowledge and identity projects worldwide.

As AI agents become increasingly capable, the line between humans and machines grows harder to see. Billions solves this by establishing a verifiable trust layer — for both people and AI — that works across borders, devices, and platforms.

## What Billions Enables

### For Humans
Billions lets individuals verify their identity without exposing personal information. Users receive **Official Profile badges** — Basic, Verified, or Premium — that signal credibility across any platform in the network.

- Prove you are a real person, not a bot
- Access rewards and gated experiences for verified users
- Own your identity without relying on a centralized authority

### For AI Agents
Through **Know Your Agent (KYA)**, Billions extends the same trust framework to AI. Any autonomous agent can be given a verifiable identity that makes clear who built it, what it does, and who is responsible for its actions.

- Verifiable agent identity and reputation
- Accountability in multi-agent and agentic systems
- The trust layer for the emerging agentic economy

## How It Works

Billions uses **zero-knowledge technology** to confirm identity without exposing data. The process is:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Scan">
    Open the Billions app and complete a one-time identity scan from your mobile device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    Your identity is cryptographically verified. No personal data leaves your device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use">
    Your verified credential is reusable across any platform connected to the Billions network.
  </Step>
</Steps>
