## Overview

The Billions Web Wallet is the browser-based interface to your Billions identity. Available at [wallet.billions.network](https://wallet.billions.network), it gives you full access to your verified credentials, profile, and the Billions network — without needing to install anything.

## What You Can Do

- **View your profile** — see your verification status and Official Profile badge (Basic, Verified, or Premium)
- **Manage credentials** — access and share your zero-knowledge credentials with connected platforms
- **Connect to apps** — authorize third-party applications to verify your identity using Login with Billions

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the wallet">
    Navigate to [wallet.billions.network](https://wallet.billions.network) in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Connect using your Billions account. If you haven't verified yet, you'll be prompted to complete verification via the mobile app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore your identity">
    View your profile badge, credentials, and connected applications.
  </Step>
</Steps>
