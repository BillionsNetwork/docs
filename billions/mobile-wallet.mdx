## Overview

The Billions mobile app is the primary way to verify your identity and manage your credentials. It is available on iOS and Android and is designed from the ground up for privacy — your personal data never leaves your device.

## Features

- **One-scan verification** — complete your identity verification with a single scan, no special hardware required
- **Zero-knowledge proofs** — your identity is confirmed cryptographically without exposing any personal information
- **Credential management** — store and selectively share verified credentials across platforms
- **Rewards** — verified users unlock rewards and exclusive access within the Billions ecosystem
